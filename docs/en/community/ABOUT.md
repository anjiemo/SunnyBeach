# About SunnyBeach

SunnyBeach was founded on November 22, 2014, on a sunny afternoon.

Inspired by a line from Han Han's movie "The Continent": "sun of beach," the SunnyBeach community was born. SunnyBeach is a community website dedicated to learning programming.

Here you can learn, write blogs, take notes, share experiences, ask questions, and share links. You can meet like-minded people and gain knowledge, experience, and genuine friendship.

## Our Mission

Make learning programming easier.

## Our Vision

Help every young person who loves programming become an excellent programmer.
